FAQ

Common questions before ordering.

How long does it take to build a project?

Simple projects can take 2–5 days. Advanced custom projects can take 1–3 weeks depending on parts and features.

Can I request my own custom project?

Yes. You can send your idea through the custom project form and discuss features on WhatsApp.

Do you provide delivery?

Delivery can be discussed based on your location. Pickup and local delivery options can be arranged.

Are the prices fixed?

No. Custom project prices depend on components, size, features and deadline. Ready product prices are starting prices.

Do you provide support after buying?

Basic testing support is available. Damage caused by wrong power, water, short circuits or burned boards is not covered.
