Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive the files?
After purchase, you will instantly receive a download link via Payhip and email containing a ZIP file with all project assets.
Can a beginner build these?
Yes! We provide difficulty ratings. "Easy" and "Medium" projects come with highly detailed, beginner-friendly wiring guides.
Are physical components included?
No, these are digital bundles (Code, Diagrams, Reports, Guides). You will need to purchase the hardware components locally.
